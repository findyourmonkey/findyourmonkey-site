Official position

U.S. Army doctrine (FM 4-0, 2024) defines four levels of warfare: national strategic (political), theater strategic, operational, and tactical.

“The levels of warfare distinguish four broad overlapping activities: providing national direction and creating national strategy (national strategic); conducting continuous theater campaigning (theater strategic); planning and conducting campaigns and major operations (operational); or planning and executing operations, battles, engagements, and actions (tactical).”
Government / court record FM4-0-MTTBaselineBrief-revised-091330SEP24.pdf

Problem with it

Some doctrinal skeptics argue the three-level framework artificially separates political strategy from military execution, but this acknowledges the political dimension rather than denying it.

“critics argue that the rigid stratification of war into three levels artificially separates political strategy from military execution”
