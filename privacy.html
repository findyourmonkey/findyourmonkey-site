Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FindYourMonkey ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our automated content pipeline service and associated social media accounts.

Information We Collect

We do not ask visitors to this website for personal information. Our automated content pipeline posts short-form video content to social media platforms on our own creator accounts. We do not collect, store, or process data from other users.

TikTok Integration

Our application uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to post video content to our own TikTok creator account (@findyourmonkey). We do not access, collect, or store any TikTok user data. We do not use TikTok login or any user-facing TikTok features.

Cookies

This website uses Google AdSense, which uses cookies. We do not set any cookies of our own.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google My Ad Center, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-Party Services

We use the following platforms to distribute content: YouTube, TikTok, Rumble, X (Twitter), and Threads. Each platform has its own privacy policy governing your use of their services.

Data Retention

Our content pipeline keeps no user data. Game data is kept while your tables are active and deleted on request.

Mom's Basement (our tabletop game)

Our game at relay.findyourmonkey.com/dnd keeps what is needed to run your games: your characters, the story and chat of each table, an optional account name, friend code and PIN, and any feedback or voice notes you choose to send us. We use it only to run and improve the game. We do not sell it. Ads shown in the game, if any, are non-personalized. To have your data deleted, email us.
