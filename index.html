Why ours are different

The machine checks its own work, so you don't have to.

Anything can chop a video into pieces. These are the things that make a clip unpostable, and every one is checked on every clip before you ever see it.

Starts where the thought starts

Clips never open or close mid-sentence, and the title quotes words actually spoken in that clip.

Your sponsor never gets clipped

Ad reads are found and cut out before anything is made, so a promo code never ends up in a Short.

Captions match the audio

Every finished clip is transcribed again and compared to its own captions. Anything that drifted is rejected.

Somebody is always on screen

The crop follows a real person and is re-checked at every camera cut, not once per clip.

Questions

The short answers.

Is the free clip really free?
Yes. Send us a link to your show and we'll cut a clip from a real episode and send it to you. No card, no call, no obligation. If you like it, we talk about the rest.
What do I need to send you?
Just a link: your podcast, YouTube channel or RSS feed. We take it from there. For a course, send whatever you already have: videos, a book, episodes or notes.
Do I have to sign a contract?
No. Clips are month to month and you can stop any month. The archive's monthly fee can be cancelled whenever. Courses cost nothing unless they sell. Full terms.
Will it sound like my show?
That's the point. Clips start and end where your thoughts do, titles quote what was actually said, and you pick the look. You approve the first batch before anything goes out.
Is a person involved, or is it all AI?
Both. Our machines do the finding, cutting, captioning and checking, around the clock. A real person, Chris, runs the studio, answers your messages and makes the calls a machine shouldn't.
Who owns what you make?
You do. The clips, the transcripts, the course and its students and money are all yours. Course payments go straight into your own Stripe account.
Do you post the clips for me?
We can. Most shows post their own, but we'll schedule and post them for you, and flag comments worth answering, for $350 a month.
I don't have a podcast. Can you still help?
Probably. If something in your work is repetitive, a machine can usually do it. We've built inventory apps, pricing tools, games, promos and music. Tell us what eats your time.
